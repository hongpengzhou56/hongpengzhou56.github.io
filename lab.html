HealthAI Lab

Efficient and interpretable AI for biomedical discovery.

HealthAI Lab is led by Dr Hongpeng Zhou in the Department of Computer Science at The University of Manchester. We develop machine learning methods to understand complex biomedical data and the biological mechanisms they reveal.

Research directions

01

Machine learning for biomedicine

AI methods for single-cell transcriptomics, spatial omics and imaging mass cytometry, including foundation models and batch effect correction.

02

Interpretable & Bayesian learning

Sparse Bayesian methods, interpretable neural networks and learning approaches that reveal structure and mechanisms in data.

03

Efficient learning & dynamical systems

Neural architecture search, network compression and system identification, connecting machine learning with scientific and engineering applications.

Our people

Our group brings together postdoctoral researchers, PhD students and collaborators working across machine learning, biomedical science and engineering.
