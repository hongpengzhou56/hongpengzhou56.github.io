PhD funding & applications

Funding schemes have their own eligibility requirements and deadlines. Check the scheme’s current call and contact us to discuss project availability. Self-funded and part-time PhD enquiries are also welcome.

  • President’s Doctoral Scholarship
  • Dean's Doctoral Scholarship
  • The University of Manchester-China Scholarship Council (UoM-CSC) Joint Scholarship (Chinese Students only, check a backup link here)

English Requirements should be satisfied if you want to apply for PhD positions. Please don’t hesitate to contact me if you have any questions about the application.
