Short bio

I am a Dame Kathleen Ollerenshaw Fellow (Assistant Professor) in the Department of Computer Science at The University of Manchester.

I lead HealthAI Lab, where we develop interpretable, efficient and practically useful AI methods for biomedical research. Our aim is to uncover the biological mechanisms behind complex biomedical data and help translate machine learning into biomedical discovery.

My research interests include machine learning, sparse Bayesian learning, interpretable learning and bioinformatics.

Background

2021 – 2023

Postdoctoral Research Associate · University of Manchester

I was a postdoc research associate at Prof.Magnus Rattray's group - Division of Informatics, Imaging and Data Sciences at the Faculty of Biology, Medicine and Health of the University of Manchester. My research project was to develop machine learning algorithms to reveal biological mechanisms behind single-cell transcriptomics data.

2018 – 2022

PhD · Delft University of Technology

Before the postdoc, I received my PhD degree at the Department of Cognitive Robotics of the Delft University of Technology in the Netherlands, supervised by Prof.Martijn Wisse and Dr.Wei Pan. My PhD work was developing Bayesian deep learning algorithms for neural network compression, neural architecture search, interpretable deep learning and system identification. Please check my PhD thesis "Bayesian deep learning for system identification".

2011 – 2017

Bachelor’s & Master’s · Harbin Institute of Technology

I received my Bachelor's and Master's degrees at the Department of Control Science and Engineering of Harbin Institute of Technology in China, supervised by Prof.Shen Yin.
